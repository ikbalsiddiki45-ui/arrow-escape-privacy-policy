1. Introduction

Welcome to Arrow Escape, developed by Nexabytes Studio ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe, transparent, and enjoyable gaming experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information, device permissions, and third-party advertising in compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act), and international privacy regulations.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

Arrow Escape does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, physical address, phone number, email address, contact list, location, microphone audio, camera, or photos.

A. Locally Stored Game Data

All your game progress (such as unlocked puzzle levels, remaining hearts, high scores, and audio/vibration preferences) is stored exclusively on your device's local storage (HTML5 LocalStorage / Android SharedPreferences). This information stays strictly on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.

B. Automatically Processed Information via Advertising

To provide free gameplay, Arrow Escape integrates third-party advertising via Google AdMob. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personal device information, including:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
  • Coarse network connection type (Wi-Fi, cellular)
  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) for ad serving and frequency capping
  • Basic non-personal diagnostic data and ad interaction events (impressions, clicks)

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with trusted third-party services that process information in accordance with their independent privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob & Google Play Services: Used for displaying banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.
    Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
    How Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Device Permissions

Arrow Escape requests only the minimum permissions strictly necessary to operate:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback during puzzle interactions and mistakes. This is processed entirely on your device.
  • android.permission.INTERNET: Used to communicate with Google AdMob servers to display ad creatives and check game connectivity.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to check if an active internet connection is available before attempting to load ad creatives.

The app does not access camera, microphone, GPS location, contacts, or storage files.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Arrow Escape is designed for general audiences and puzzle lovers of all ages. We take children's privacy seriously:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
  • The application explicitly sets Google Mobile Ads child-directed treatment (setTagForChildDirectedTreatment(TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT_TRUE)) to ensure non-personalized, family-safe advertising.
  • If a parent or guardian becomes aware that information has been provided to us inadvertently, please contact us immediately at ikbalsiddiki45@gmail.com and we will take prompt steps to address it.

6. Opt-Out and Advertising ID Controls

You have complete control over your advertising identifiers. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time through your Android device settings:

Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

7. Data Security

Because all game data remains local to your device, your progress is as secure as your device itself. Any network communication handled by third-party ad libraries is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.
